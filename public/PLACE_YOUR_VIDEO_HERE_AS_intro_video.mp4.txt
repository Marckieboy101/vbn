To have your uploaded blonde avatar video (or any custom introduction video) play automatically in the application:

1. Rename your local video file to exactly "intro_video.mp4"
2. Place it in this exact folder: public/
3. Rebuild or run the application.

If there is no "intro_video.mp4" file in this folder, the application will automatically fall back to the high-quality cinematic projector film trailer during execution.
